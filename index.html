01 The experience

The world does not wait to become real.

Five connected principles make a compact place more believable, more legible, and more consequential.

  1. 01

    Living Consequence

    What happens can become part of what happens next. Choices matter through the reality they alter, not through a score displayed afterward.

  2. 02

    Living Characters

    People continue with knowledge, motives, relationships, obligations, and limits of their own. They are participants in the world, not dispensers waiting for the player.

  3. 03

    Dense Discovery

    The territory grows through understanding. Familiar places reveal new meaning as circumstances change and connections become clearer.

  4. 04

    Maramis Made Real

    Culture, material life, history, environment, and myth form one coherent identity—not generic fantasy wearing an Arabian surface.

  5. 05

    Embodied Presence

    First-person physicality, performance, sound, and atmosphere place the player inside events while preserving presence and agency.

03 Living Worldcraft

Activity is not enough.
Cause must survive.

MAVYR does not simulate motion merely to make the world appear busy. It preserves meaningful realities strongly enough that an absence, discovery, decision, or intervention can alter what becomes possible later.

04 Maramis

Grounded enough to believe.
Mythic enough to awe.

Maramis is the larger universe to which MAVYR belongs. Its identity is built through the life of a place: architecture and landscape, clothing and objects, work and language, social structure and history, myth and supernatural reality.

The extraordinary matters because it emerges from a world with weight before spectacle arrives. The aim is not to explain all of Maramis. It is to make one part of it feel specific enough that you want to know it.

05 The territory

One place,
known deeply.

MAVYR grows through familiarity, discovery, revisitation, and changing circumstances—not geographic multiplication.

  1. 01 The social heart Primary Settlement
  2. 02 The connective tissue Connected Wilderness / Routes
  3. 03 The deep destination Major Ruin / Deep Destination
The goal is not to visit many places. It is to come to know one place deeply.

06 Craft & direction

Weighted. Intimate. Grounded.

Handcrafted Stylized Realism MAVYR targets culturally specific forms, readable material life, and atmosphere with physical credibility.

Embodied First Person Physical presentation, signature audio, and selective performance excellence concentrate craft where it will be felt most.

Premium Cohesion The standard is cohesion—not scale for its own sake, and not spectacle detached from the world beneath it.
